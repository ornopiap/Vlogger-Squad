🎬 Vlog Squad

Start a video call with your friends. It opens in Jitsi Meet, which works across networks.

Everyone must use the exact same room name, or the same invite link. Copying the invite link adds a random ending so strangers can’t guess your room.

In the call

  • Active speaker: the default view switches to whoever is talking.
  • Everyone at once: tap the tile-view button (grid icon) in the bottom bar.
  • A few people: pin a person to keep them on screen.
  • Use headphones to avoid echo.

Recording the vlog

  • Easiest way: one person records their screen while in the call (Windows: Win+G, Mac: Shift+Cmd+5, phone: built-in screen recorder).
  • Jitsi’s own Record option, if it appears in the “More” menu, may also work.
  • Ask everyone to stay in the view you want captured.
